# AIBOT ROADMAP 2 — "bots that move like players" (APPROVED 2 Sep 2026 — rulings in §5)

Founder brief (2 Sep): audit the whole AI bot; fix what we see; make it more advanced. Observed:
(1) a bot stranded on a raised platform turns in place "looking for a target" instead of leaving
the platform and roaming the level; (2) several bots pile onto one target and jam against each
other; (3) teammates collide and knock each other around; (4) everyone takes the same shortest
path and moves in a line. Wanted: roaming with random points until a target is seen, then go for
it like a human; a per-target attacker cap (2v1, the third looks elsewhere) with team awareness;
teammate proximity avoidance; tactical path variety; goal + utility AI, "advanced". Deliver a
phased plan, by WAVES, with the crew — and show it before applying.

Sources: crew inventory of `Plugins/AIBot` (this session), technique research brief (this
session, 40 cited sources), `docs/AIBOT-ROADMAP.md` (phases 1–10, done), `docs/AIBOT-WAVES.md`
(W-AUDIT / W-BUILD / W-REVIEW / W-VERIFY), `Plugins/AIBot/Source/AIBot/FAIRPLAY.md` (F1–F8).

## 1. TARGET ARCHITECTURE — goal + utility, three layers, one shared team mind

```
                 ┌──────────────── SERVER ONLY ────────────────┐
  Perception ──► Sensorium ──► FACTS ──► AMBITION (utility)  ──► TACTIC (utility) ──► ACTION (StateTree task)
   (sight/hear)   (memory)      │         Roam · Engage ·          Push · Flank ·        MoveTo(filter) · RunEQS ·
                                │         Retreat · Objective      Hold · Explore        Claim/Release · Verb(tag)
                                ▼
                        AIB TEAM MIND (new, per team, UWorldSubsystem, not replicated)
                        · shared last-known enemy positions (decaying)
                        · TARGET CLAIMS with cap (2) + hysteresis + timed expiry
                        · VISIT HEAT GRID (coarse, decayed) for exploration
                        · ROUTE HEAT GRID (cells my teammates' active paths cross)
                        · ally positions (already accepted debt, 27 Aug)
```

Design rules: utility everywhere a choice is made (ambition, tactic, target, roam point), a
predictable StateTree for execution, every weight in `Content/Data/AIB_*.csv` (law 3), every
random draw from a seeded `FRandomStream` (match seed + bot index) so a headless replay repeats,
every decision logged as a structured `LogAIBot` line the metrics script already parses.

## 2. PHASES (each = one packet: W-AUDIT → W-BUILD → W-REVIEW → W-VERIFY, per AIBOT-WAVES.md)

### Phase 11 — EGRESS: nobody spins on a platform (defect 1)  [highest impact, lowest cost]
BUILD  · Nav: keep generator links, widen `BN_Drop` JumpLength to the measured platform gaps;
         blockout scripts (`Tools/blockout/*`) place `ANavLinkProxy` at every platform edge the
         generator misses (script-generated, law 7); one `UNavArea_Jump` for up-links.
       · Controller: `UAIBPathFollowingComponent` (subclass, `SetMoveSegment`) presses the JUMP
         verb when a segment enters a jump area — jump-down/up become path-following, not a
         wedge watchdog side effect.
       · Brain: `FAIBIslandFacts` — "can I reach the team centroid / any roam sample?" computed
         once per pump (one `TestPathSync`); a NEW tactic `Egress` under Roam that walks to the
         nearest island edge and steps off (drop link or authored proxy). `SweepLook` gets a
         budget (`SweepMaxSeconds`, csv) and fails into Roam → Egress; it never runs forever.
       · Roam: EQS `PathingGrid` + custom `UEnvQueryTest_VisitRecency` scoring against the team
         VISIT HEAT GRID; random reachable points across the WHOLE level, not a 2500uu disc.
REVIEW · aib-critic attack surfaces: fairness (no omniscient teleport-to-nav), utility pathologies
         (Egress/Roam ping-pong), containment (plugin boundary grep).
VERIFY · headless seeded 4v4 on Spillway + Arena01: `stuck_seconds` per bot, `no_path_requests`,
         `sweep_seconds`, `island_egress_count`; PIE watch of a bot spawned on the top platform
         leaving it within 5 s. Proof: `AIBot.Sim.Egress`, metrics gate.

### Phase 12 — TEAM MIND + TARGET CLAIMS: 2v1, the third looks elsewhere (defect 2)
BUILD  · `UAIBTeamMind` (server `UWorldSubsystem`, per team): shared sightings (last-known
         position + stamp, decaying by F5), `FAIBTargetClaims` — `TryClaim(bot, target, cap=2)`,
         release on Engage exit / target death (gameplay-tag event, no poll) / timed expiry.
       · `FAIBTargetPolicy::Score` gains an `AlliesOnTarget` term: ×1/(1+n), 0 at cap; keeps the
         incumbent bonus + switch margin (hysteresis) so claims do not thrash.
       · Engage goal spread: the approach goal is a ring sample around the belief, seeded per
         bot, so two attackers come from two sides (this alone breaks the jam at 350uu).
       · The claims board's "agents are never claimable" rule is REPLACED deliberately, in its own
         packet, with the cap as the new invariant (documented in FAIRPLAY as amendment).
REVIEW · aib-critic: fairness (shared sightings must obey F2/F5 — no bot learns what no teammate
         saw), claim thrash, the third bot's fallback (must not idle).
VERIFY · `target_pileup_count` == 0 by construction; `claim_grant/deny` in the metrics script;
         headless A/B vs Phase 11 (kills/min must not drop). Proof: `AIBot.Sim.Claims` extended.

### Phase 13 — SEPARATION: teammates keep their space (defect 3)
BUILD  · Detour Crowd on the bot controller (`UCrowdFollowingComponent` as the path-following
         subobject, separation on, weight from csv), players registered as crowd obstacles, NOT
         RVO (mixing is forbidden; RVO leaves the mesh). The Phase 11 jump hook moves onto the
         crowd component subclass (Detour halts at custom links otherwise — known trap).
       · Wedge watchdog learns the difference between geometry and a teammate: a teammate within
         the capsule sum yields (wait/side-step) instead of jumping.
       · Pawn physics interaction off between bots (no shoving).
REVIEW · aib-critic: containment (crowd component is plugin-side), server-only (avoidance never
         runs on clients), replication unchanged.
VERIFY · `teammate_overlap_events` (>0.25 s), `mean_pairwise_teammate_distance`; the listen-server
         rung (host + one client) confirms movement replication — the three-way claim.

### Phase 14 — ROUTE VARIETY: tactical paths, no conga line (defect 4)
BUILD  · Lane `UNavArea` classes tagged by the blockout scripts (`NavModifierVolume`, script-placed);
         `UAIBQueryFilter` per bot with seeded lane weights (clamped ±30 %) passed through
         `FAIMoveRequest::SetNavigationFilter`.
       · ROUTE HEAT GRID: a bot stamps the cells of its active path; teammates' filters add cost
         there (virtual filter only when a teammate is routing; off for Roam).
       · Engage tactics by utility: `Push` (direct), `Flank` (EQS mid-point off the line and
         hidden from the target, then the target), `Hold` (fight range station). Weights csv.
REVIEW · aib-critic: utility pathologies (visible stupid detours), fairness (flank points must be
         reachable and seen-from nothing the bot has not sensed).
VERIFY · `path_share_ratio` per pair, flank usage share, kills/min A/B vs Phase 13.

### Phase 15 — BRAIN ON UTILITY SELECTORS (the "advanced" layer, after 11–14 are measured)
BUILD  · Ambition and tactic selection move onto StateTree utility selectors (Try Select Children
         With Highest Utility) with C++ Considerations reading the Team Mind, the pawn's GAS
         attributes (read-only), and csv response curves; `MinDwell` per ambition; seeded random
         for weighted selection. Tree still generated by `UAIBTreeAuthoring`.
       · Visit heat grid grows into a modular influence map (visited / enemy presence / threat)
         ONLY if Phase 11 telemetry says roaming still looks aimless.
REVIEW · aib-critic on oscillation and determinism; aib-verifier replays a seeded match twice and
         diffs the decision log (must match).
VERIFY · all six metrics vs Phase 14; human playtest rung (founder) — bots "move like players".

### Phase 16 — HYGIENE (parallel, low risk): delete the dead BR/BN bot systems, rewrite
`ai-builder.md` (it describes code that does not exist), give every AIB ticket a STATUS line, land
`Breachpoint.Bots.*` as the named suite the quality bars expect, unblock Gauntlet or record why.

## 5. RULINGS (founder, 2 Sep 2026 — the plan is approved on these)

1. Capped claims on pawns: YES — FAIRPLAY amendment to be written in Phase 12's packet.
2. Team-shared sightings: ALL tiers. "Forget about training or anything like that" — there is ONE
   target tier, Spartan. The tier table stays as data but every bot ships at the top; awareness
   (teammates, enemies, environment) is never gated by tier.
3. Script-placed `NavLinkProxy` in the blockout maps: YES.
4. Detour Crowd owns steering during moves; strafing is welcome ("as long as you're moving").
5. Order: 11 → 12 → 13 → 14 → 15, Phase 16 alongside.

**LAW F9 — MOTION IS THE DEFAULT** (founder: "what we don't want is to have AI just standing
still... ever... unless there is a tactical way of standing still"). A bot is either moving, or
executing a named tactic that requires stillness (Hold at fight range, an ability wind-up, a
reload behind cover) for a bounded time. Every phase adds `idle_seconds` to its metrics: seconds
with no locomotion input AND no active stillness-tactic; the gate is `idle_seconds == 0`
outside named tactics. `SweepLook` in place is therefore illegal from Phase 11 on: a search sweeps
WHILE moving. Standing to "look for a target" is exactly the defect and is banned by name.

**"As advanced as possible."** Phases 11–15 are the floor, not the ceiling; each W-AUDIT lists
what the phase could add beyond its fix (abilities/actions used tactically, environment awareness
— ledges, sightlines, pickups — and teammate intent), and the aib-critic scores those additions
for fairness before they are built.
