# Halo Infinite multiplayer map research — the digest behind Arena 02 (BN28)

> Compiled 29 Aug 2026 (cloud lead, research agent over web sources). Facts carry
> sources; gaps are DECLARED, not filled. Full per-map notes in the ticket log's
> provenance; this file keeps what the arena design actually used.

## The synthesis Arena 02 is built on

No shipped Infinite map is symmetric + three-level + grapple-first at once, so the
design is a documented hybrid: **AQUARIUS' plan** (the only pure small symmetric 4v4
343 shipped: "three major hallways and two open arenas", two-exit bases, exact
pickup mirroring — Ketul Majmudar's level-design analysis + Gfinity/dotesports
callout guides) **x RECHARGE's section** (343's only small map "all about
verticality": three floors, a circulation ring that changes level as it goes,
center-bottom power pit, corner-to-corner bridges — Gfinity/Halopedia), with
**STREETS' sightline discipline** ("endless ledges… create fairly limited
sightlines", one readable silhouette per route — Cayle George, IGN First) and
**CATALYST as the existence proof** that symmetric + vertical + grapple-rewarding
ships (Halo Waypoint Season 2 preview).

## The rules the manifest encodes (each traceable)

1. Top power pickup at exact center, elevated, equidistant, visible from most of
   the map (Aquarius Top Mid, Bazaar Market, Live Fire Turbine) — our rocket node,
   deck's south lip, map center in x.
2. The long-range prize sits on the most exposed direct lane (Live Fire's sniper
   Hallway; Empyrean's mid-corridor rockets) — our Gallery is the one long lane.
3. Symmetric maps mirror pickups exactly ("whatever is on one side is on the
   other, flipped" — Bazaar) — the manifest is EMITTED through a mirror function.
4. Skeleton = three lanes + two arenas (Aquarius, literally) — Gallery / center
   sandwich / Works, two courtyards.
5. Junction splits should offer DIFFERENT LEVELS, not just left/right (Majmudar) —
   every courtyard junction offers ground lane, stairs to deck, or grapple up.
6. One deliberate long line, everything else broken by construction (Streets) —
   Gallery 26 m clear; streets broken 13/22/13; culvert baffled.
7. Height buys information, not safety (Live Fire Tower "exposed from all
   directions"; Catalyst's coverless light bridge) — the Crossing carries no cover.
8. Bases get two+ exits, both funneling toward mid (Aquarius) — canopy bays open
   three ways.
9. Grapple owns the mid-tier of verticality (Paul Crocker, Game Developer;
   Fragmentation makes high ground grapple-only) — z8 is grapple-only, GDD 2.6.
10. Traversal metrics gate heights: 343's own Forge guidance — jump 8 : clamber 12
    : grapple 80 units (Halo Support, Community Forge Map Requirements). Our 4 m
    floors sit above BN's jump, well inside one 20 m grapple.

## Numbers that survived scrutiny

- Ranked item cadence: Camo/OS 0:30 / 1:30 / then 2:30; sword 1:30 then 2:30
  (Steam guide 2661245554) — precedent for our fixed 90 s rocket (GDD).
- BR75 optimal TTK 1.55 s (blueberries.gg) — why Halo tolerates ~30 m lines.
- 4v4 competitive footprints are UNPUBLISHED (declared gap). Bounding data:
  Behemoth 172x174 m "plays like BTB", Deadlock 122x122 (ExpertBeacon) — sane 4v4
  band ~60-90 m. Arena 02 is 56x40 — tight-side deliberate, tuned to a 35 m cap
  and an AR/Magnum sandbox rather than BR cross-map poke.
- Grapple range claims conflict (8 m TheGamer vs 12-15 m Game Informer vs 80
  units official) — the RATIO is the reliable part; BN's own 20 m (GDD) governs.

## Explicitly NOT copied

- Aquarius' full-length base-to-base sightline (violates our 35 m cap).
- Camo/Overshield as pickups (the slice ships rocket-only power — GDD 6).
- Clamber (BN has none: stairs carry L0-L1, the hook carries the rest).
