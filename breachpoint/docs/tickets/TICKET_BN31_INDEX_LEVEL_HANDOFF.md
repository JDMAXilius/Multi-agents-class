## What is NOT proven, and is your job

The cloud proved GEOMETRY only. Not proven: the pawn actually walks it, nav
mesh generates, the map is fun, and — the founder's own caution — that any
of it is truly faithful to Aquarius. Assume the cloud's version may be
wrong; your eyes in the engine against the reference images outrank it.

## Rules that apply to all four tickets

- Law 7: blockouts are **generated by committed scripts**, never hand-placed.
  A hand fix in the .umap is lost on the next rebuild — fix the GENERATOR.
- Law 6 (honesty ladder): every "works" names its rung. "The script ran" is
  not "the level plays".
- Decisions go in the ticket Log, not in chat. A decision that lives only in
  chat is lost.
- Claim a ticket with a STATUS line, commit + push the claim, then work.
