## Why this exists

The level is 292 placed pieces of a 15-asset kit. Today the builder falls
back to scaling `/Engine/BasicShapes/Cube`. That works, but it is not a KIT:
you cannot edit "the wall" once and have every wall change. This ticket
makes the fifteen real assets so the level is genuinely modular.

## The spec (do not re-derive it — read it)

- `Content/Data/blockout_kit_assets.json` — machine-readable: 15 entries,
  each with `id`, `asset_path`, `shape`, `size_m`, `scales` (what may be

- `docs/design/blueprints/breachpoint_aquarius/K101/K102/K103_kit_catalog_*.png`
  — the drawn catalog, one card per asset with dimensions and scaling rule.

The fifteen: Floor_400, Wall_400, HalfWall_200, Doorway_400, Ramp_800,
Stair_200, Column_100, Pier_100, Crate_100, Battery_240, Rail_400,
Curb_400, Wall45_200, GlassWall_400, Pedestal_120.

## Rules that are LAW here

- **Sizes are exact.** Author at the `size_m` in the spec. Do not "round to
  something nicer" — the level's 292 placements compute their scale as
  `desired / asset size`, so a wrong base size silently mis-sizes the map.
- **Pivots:** footprint CENTRE, base at z=0. Exception: `BLK_Floor_400`
  centres in Z (it is a plate placed by its centre).
- **Never-scale assets:** Stair_200, Battery_240, Pedestal_120, and the
  Doorway's OPENING. Rise:run and clearances are gameplay (research).
- Every asset: **simple collision** (box; convex for the Ramp wedge),
  **mobility Static**, one flat grey material `/Game/Blockout/M_Blockout_Grey`.
- Naming exactly `SM_<id>` under `/Game/Blockout/Meshes/`.
- Law 7: assets are **generated by a committed script**, never hand-modelled.

## How to work (the MCP surface)

Per `.claude/skills/ue-editor` and `.claude/skills/unreal-mcp`:

1. `list_toolsets` — confirm the server is up (it only exists while the
   editor is open). Never work from a remembered tool list.
2. `describe_toolset("editor_toolset.toolsets.static_mesh.StaticMeshTools")`
   and `describe_toolset("editor_toolset.toolsets.asset.AssetTools")` —
   read the REAL input/output schemas before calling anything.
3. Remember the four failure rules: every schema property must be present
   (pass `null`/`""`, not omitted); object refs are `{"refPath": "..."}`;
   results are JSON inside `returnValue`; not every tool has an
   `outputSchema` — make one real call and look.
4. More than ~3 dependent calls: use
   `ProgrammaticToolset.execute_tool_script` (call
   `get_execution_environment` first for the contract).
