## Steps (in order)

1. **Ratify the replication shape (D21).** Owner: **lead** + **netcode-builder**. The
   recommendation on the register, for approval or replacement:
   - `UBRMotionTrackerComponent` on **`ABRPlayerController`** — one per viewer, so per-viewer
     filtering is structural rather than a filter someone can forget.
   - One replicated `TArray<FBRTrackerContact>`, `COND_OwnerOnly`, capped at `MaxContacts` from
     the data row. Bounded by construction — never every pawn's transform.
   - `FBRTrackerContact` carries **quantised bearing + distance bucket + team + kind
     (precise|edge)** — NOT a world transform. A memory-reading cheat then gets exactly what the
     ring already draws and nothing more. This is the whole anti-wallhack argument; a contact
     struct holding `FVector_NetQuantize World` fails review.
   - Server timer at **5 Hz** (matches `BREACHPOINT-QUALITY-BARS.md` §2's `BRGameState` line).
     No Tick (law 4).
   - **Zero client→server RPCs.** Server→client only; no client intent means no `_Validate`
     surface. A "request contacts" RPC in any proposal is the design being wrong.
2. **The disable is server-side.** With `bEnabled=false` the server never starts the timer and
   the array stays empty all match. A client-side "don't draw it" is not a disable — `high`.
3. **The undetected states are server-side culls.** Crouch-walk uses the CMC's `IsCrouching()`
   (already live at `BRCharacterMovementComponent.cpp:167`). The Walk-binding half is
   unimplementable today — see the gap below — and is recorded rather than faked.
4. **Row struct + table.** `FBRMotionTrackerRow` in `Data/BRDataRows.h`;
   `Content/Data/DT_MotionTracker.csv` generated by the committed reimport script, never
   hand-placed. **tuning-curator** proposes values, **netcode-builder** lands the struct.
5. **The attack ships with the feature** (netcode law 8). `BRMotionTrackerSpec.cpp` must reject:
   (a) a contact beyond precise range appearing as precise; (b) a crouched enemy appearing at
   all; (c) any contact on a `bEnabled=false` row; (d) an enemy contact reaching a non-owning
   connection; (e) a stale contact surviving seamless travel; (f) a join-in-progress client
   reading contacts before its first replication (law 7 — array arrives empty, not null-deref).
6. **Verify.** Rung 1 (three targets) + rung 2 (the spec). Rung 4 in threes — server, acting
   client, observing client — before "works" is said out loud (R30: 4b listen is REQUIRED).
7. **Critic REFUTER**, attack surface named: the contact struct's information content, the
   per-viewer cull, the Ranked/Tactical disable path, join-in-progress.
